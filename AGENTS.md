## Truth Guard
- Treat Truth Guard as a deterministic declared-evidence integrity guard, not a real-world truth oracle. Its gate always leaves real-world `truth_status=NOT_MEASURED`.
- For money, payment, contracts, deadlines, medical/legal/security matters, deletion, deployment, irreversible action, or premise confirmation, use the installed `$truth-guard-project` HIGH workflow before the final answer.
- When the project Codex hooks are active, append exactly one final `truth-guard:v1` route marker. A HIGH turn requires a passing ledger under `.truth-guard/ledgers/`; LOW/NONE must not declare a ledger.
- Never fabricate a source, capture, quote, hash, timestamp, ledger field, or PASS result. Revise to a scoped unknown or abstention when evidence is insufficient.
- Local hooks are bypassable. Do not claim merge or deployment enforcement without current CI dependency and remote protection evidence.
